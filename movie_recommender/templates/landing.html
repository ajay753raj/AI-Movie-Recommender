About Our Recommendation Engine

Welcome to Cinematch, a next-generation recommendation engine engineered for true film enthusiasts. Unlike traditional platforms that rely on simple genre tags or commercial algorithms, Cinematch uses advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze the deeper thematic elements of cinema.

Our system processes full movie descriptions, plot outlines, and thematic summaries using TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) vectorization. By converting written narratives into high-dimensional numerical vectors, we calculate the Cosine Similarity between films. This mathematical approach identifies subtle thematic, narrative, and stylistic connections that simple categories miss.
